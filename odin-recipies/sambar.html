Sambar



About Sambar

Sambar is the culinary anchor of South Indian culture. It is a lentil and vegetable stew that represents community, history, and daily life across the southern states of India.

Prepration

    cook the lentils

  1. wash the toor dal thoroughly in water
  2. pressure cook the dal with water,turmeric and a drop of oil
  3. cook until mushy
  4. mash the cooked dal and set it aside

    Extact The Tamarind

  1. soak lemon sized tamarind in warm water
  2. squeez the tamarind pulp
  3. strain the liquid
  4. discard the leftover pulp and keep the extract

    Cook The Vegetables

  1. heat a heavy bottomed pot
  2. add the chopped vegetables
  3. pour in the tamarind extract
  4. add a pinch of salt and simmer until tender
